BCS Karate is Back

Tuesdays & Thursdays 3:15pm and 4:00pm - ages 4 and up

Description

The BCS Karate Club is no longer in its “launch” season—it’s in its thriving season. With around 30 BCS students currently enrolled, this after-school program continues to grow as students train, advance, and earn their ranks right here on campus. The beauty of this program is its flexibility: students may join at any time. New students will not “catch up” to those already ahead—but they will progress the same way every karate student does, earning their rank through effort, consistency, and discipline. Schedule & Location Days: Tuesdays and Thursdays Times: Little Dragons (Younger Students): 3:15 – 4:00 PM Older Students: 4:00 – 4:45 PM Location: Faith Factory room (spacious with mirrored walls, capacity 25–30 students). Age Groups Little Dragons (Ages 4 – 4th Grade): Focus on foundational karate skills and fun activities. Older Group (5th Grade and Up): Training designed for maturity, discipline, and advanced development. Note: Final grade-level cutoffs will be adjusted based on enrollment to keep groups balanced and accommodate friendships. Pricing Options Choose the payment option that works best for your family: $75 per month (recurring payment). $200 per semester (one-time payment, discounted rate). 💡 Our pricing is set below the rate of local dojos, offering you convenience and value on campus. Uniforms Cost: $35 (one-time fee). Details: Uniforms are required for participation and can be ordered during registration. Policy: Students without uniforms on a given day will still be allowed to participate. Student Dismissal Teachers will direct all karate club students to the Faith Factory after school. Students in the Little Dragons group will be dismissed from karate directly at 3:45 PM. Older students waiting for the 3:45 PM session will remain supervised. PICK UP: From After School Care - pull under the portico and tell them your student's name. Next Steps for Families: Register now:https://pci.jotform.com/form/252366142497159.
